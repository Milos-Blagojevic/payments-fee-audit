# Payout and Foreign Exchange Services Agreement

*Illustrative contract text for a fictional company. Not a real agreement and not legal advice.*

**Agreement reference:** KP-2025-09

**Dated:** 1 September 2025

**Between:**

1. **Kestrel Payouts** (the "Provider"); and
2. **Fernway**, a marketplace payments company (the "Customer").

## Background

(A) The Provider executes payouts to bank accounts in several currencies and converts funds between currencies for that purpose.

(B) The Customer pays out marketplace sellers' funds and wishes to use the Provider's services to do so.

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement the following terms have the following meanings:

- **"Agreement"** means this agreement, including its Schedules, as amended from time to time.
- **"Applied Rate"** means the exchange rate actually applied by the Provider to convert euro into the currency of a Payout.
- **"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday on which banks are open for general business.
- **"EUR Equivalent"** means the amount of a Payout converted into euro at the Mid Rate for its Execution Hour.
- **"Execution Hour"** means the clock hour (UTC) that contains the Execution Time.
- **"Execution Time"** means the time at which the Provider executes a Payout.
- **"Invoice"** means the monthly invoice issued by the Provider under clause 5.1.
- **"Mid Rate"** means the mid-market exchange rate of a currency pair for a clock hour, as published for that hour in the hourly reference rate series maintained by the Provider and shown on its statements.
- **"Payout"** means a transfer of funds by the Provider from the Customer's account to a bank account nominated by the Customer.
- **"Spread"** means the amount by which the Applied Rate is less favourable to the Customer than the Mid Rate for the Execution Hour, expressed as a percentage of that Mid Rate.

**1.2 Interpretation.** (a) Headings do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. (b) References to clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and Schedules to this Agreement. (c) Unless stated otherwise, every reference to a time, a date or a calendar month is to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

## 2. Payout fees

**2.1 SEPA Payouts.** For each Payout in euro executed as a SEPA credit transfer (a "SEPA Payout") the Customer shall pay a fee of EUR 0.20 (twenty euro cents).

**2.2 Faster Payments Payouts.** For each Payout in pounds sterling executed through the UK Faster Payments scheme (a "Faster Payments Payout") the Customer shall pay a fee of EUR 0.30 (thirty euro cents).

**2.3 SWIFT Payouts.** For each Payout executed by international wire transfer through the SWIFT network (a "SWIFT Payout") the Customer shall pay a fee of 0.10% (ten basis points) of the EUR Equivalent of the Payout amount, subject to a minimum of EUR 8.00 (eight euro) and a maximum of EUR 40.00 (forty euro) per Payout.

## 3. Calculation of fees

**3.1 Currency.** All fees, charges and credits under this Agreement are calculated, invoiced and payable in euro (EUR), including fees on Payouts made in other currencies.

**3.2 Calculation and rounding.** Each fee and each credit is calculated separately for each Payout and for each fee component, and is rounded to the nearest whole euro cent before fees are aggregated on the Invoice. An amount of exactly half a cent is rounded up, away from zero.

## 4. Foreign exchange

**4.1 Spread cap.** (a) For each Payout in a currency other than euro the Provider converts the euro debited from the Customer's account at the Applied Rate.

(b) The Spread, measured against the Mid Rate for the Execution Hour of the Payout, shall not exceed the maximum shown below for the Payout currency:

| Payout currency | Maximum Spread |
|---|---|
| pounds sterling (GBP) | 0.40% (forty basis points) |
| US dollars (USD) | 0.40% (forty basis points) |
| Swiss francs (CHF) | 0.40% (forty basis points) |
| Polish zloty (PLN) | 0.90% (ninety basis points) |

(c) Where the Spread exceeds that maximum, the Provider shall refund to the Customer the euro debited in excess of the amount the maximum Spread allows.

**4.2 Rate disclosure.** For each Payout in a currency other than euro the Provider's statement shows the Applied Rate, the start of the hour whose Mid Rate was used and the euro debited.

## 5. Invoicing and disputes

**5.1 Monthly invoice.** Within ten Business Days after the end of each calendar month the Provider shall issue an Invoice for the fees of that month, together with a statement listing each Payout with its fee and the euro debited for it.

**5.2 Payment.** Each Invoice is payable within fourteen days of its date. The Provider may instead deduct the amount of an Invoice from funds it holds for the Customer, provided the Invoice has been issued first.

**5.3 Disputes.** The Customer may dispute an Invoice, or any fee or charge shown on it or on the accompanying statement, by written notice given within forty-five (45) days after the date of the Invoice. Any amount found to have been overcharged shall be credited on the next Invoice. Fees not disputed within that period are deemed accepted, except in the case of manifest error or fraud.

## 6. Commencement and duration

**6.1 Commencement.** This Agreement takes effect on 1 September 2025 (the "Effective Date") and applies to all Payouts executed on or after the Effective Date.

**6.2 Duration.** This Agreement continues for an initial term of twenty-four months from the Effective Date and afterwards until terminated by either party on three months' written notice to the other.

## 7. General

**7.1 Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes any earlier arrangement.

**7.2 Variation.** No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is made in writing and signed by or on behalf of both parties, including by an amendment letter from the Provider countersigned by the Customer. An amendment letter states the date from which it applies.

**7.3 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.
