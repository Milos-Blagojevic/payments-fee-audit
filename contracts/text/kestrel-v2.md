Dear Sir or Madam,

**Payout and Foreign Exchange Services Agreement dated 1 September 2025, reference KP-2025-09 (the "Agreement")**

We refer to the Agreement. Terms defined in the Agreement have the same meaning in this letter, and references to clauses are to clauses of the Agreement. This letter is Amendment No. 1 to the Agreement and is made under clause 7.2.

1. **Amendment Effective Date.** The amendments in paragraph 2 take effect on 1 April 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date") and apply to all Payouts executed on or after that date, determined in accordance with clause 1.2. Payouts executed before the Amendment Effective Date remain subject to the Agreement as it stood before this letter.

2. **Amendments.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date:

(a) the following new clause 2.4 is inserted after clause 2.3:

> **2.4 Elixir Payouts.** For each Payout in Polish zloty to a bank account in Poland executed through the Elixir domestic clearing system (an "Elixir Payout") the Customer shall pay a fee of EUR 0.30 (thirty euro cents).

3. **Continuation.** Except as amended by this letter, the Agreement continues in full force and effect, and every other clause, including the fees, the rounding rules and the dispute period, applies unchanged. From the Amendment Effective Date, references to the Agreement are to the Agreement as amended by this letter.

4. **Governing law.** This letter is governed by the law that governs the Agreement.
