# Master Acquiring Services Agreement

*Illustrative contract text for a fictional company. Not a real agreement and not legal advice.*

**Agreement reference:** NG-2025-01

**Dated:** 1 July 2025

**Between:**

1. **Northgate Acquiring** (the "Acquirer"); and
2. **Fernway**, a marketplace payments company (the "Merchant").

## Background

(A) The Acquirer provides card acquiring services for Visa and Mastercard payments.

(B) The Merchant operates an online marketplace and wishes to appoint the Acquirer to acquire card payments made by buyers on that marketplace, on interchange-plus-plus pricing.

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement the following terms have the following meanings:

- **"Agreement"** means this agreement, including its Schedules, as amended from time to time.
- **"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday on which banks are open for general business.
- **"Card Scheme"** means Visa or Mastercard.
- **"Chargeback"** means the reversal of a Successful Transaction initiated by the card issuer under the Card Scheme rules.
- **"Interchange Fee"** means the fee payable to the card issuer under the Card Scheme rules for a Transaction.
- **"Invoice"** means the monthly invoice issued by the Acquirer under clause 7.1.
- **"Markup"** means the Acquirer's margin payable under clause 4.2.
- **"Monthly Processed Volume"** means the aggregate amount of all Successful Transactions processed by the Acquirer for the Merchant in a calendar month, before deduction of any fee, Refund or Chargeback.
- **"Refund"** means the reversal, at the Merchant's request, of the whole amount of a Successful Transaction.
- **"Scheme Fee"** means the fee charged by a Card Scheme for a Transaction.
- **"Successful Transaction"** means a Transaction that is authorised by the card issuer and captured.
- **"Transaction"** means a payment by card to the Merchant submitted to the Acquirer for authorisation.

**1.2 Interpretation.** (a) Headings do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. (b) References to clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and Schedules to this Agreement. (c) Unless stated otherwise, every reference to a time, a date or a calendar month is to the local time in Frankfurt am Main, Germany (IANA time zone Europe/Berlin, including summer time where it applies), so that a calendar month begins at 00:00 local time on its first day.

## 2. Commencement and duration

**2.1 Commencement.** This Agreement takes effect on 1 July 2025 (the "Effective Date") and applies to all Transactions made on or after the Effective Date.

**2.2 Duration.** This Agreement continues for an initial term of twenty-four months from the Effective Date and afterwards until terminated by either party on three months' written notice to the other.

## 3. Interchange and scheme fees

**3.1 Pass-through fees.** For each Successful Transaction the Merchant shall pay the Interchange Fee and the Scheme Fee actually incurred by the Acquirer, passed through at cost without any margin. The amounts charged shall not exceed those in the interchange and scheme fee reference table designated "interchange_table_v1" and described in Schedule 1 (the "Reference Table"). The Markup under clause 4.2 is payable in addition.

## 4. Acquirer fees

**4.1 Currency.** All fees, charges and credits under this Agreement are calculated, invoiced and payable in euro (EUR).

**4.2 Markup.** (a) For each Successful Transaction the Merchant shall pay a Markup equal to the percentage of the Transaction amount shown below for the band into which the Monthly Processed Volume of the calendar month of the Transaction falls:

| Band | Monthly Processed Volume | Markup |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | less than EUR 2,500,000.00 | 0.45% (forty-five basis points) |
| 2 | EUR 2,500,000.00 or more but less than EUR 8,000,000.00 | 0.35% (thirty-five basis points) |
| 3 | EUR 8,000,000.00 or more | 0.28% (twenty-eight basis points) |

(b) The band is determined once the Monthly Processed Volume of the month is known, and its rate applies to every Successful Transaction of that month, including Transactions processed before the volume reached the lower limit of that band.

(c) The Markup is subject to the monthly minimum in clause 7.4 and rounded in accordance with clause 4.4.

**4.3 Fixed fee.** In addition to the Markup, the Merchant shall pay a fixed fee of EUR 0.04 (four euro cents) for each Successful Transaction. No fixed fee is payable on a Transaction that is declined or fails.

**4.4 Calculation and rounding.** Each fee and each credit is calculated separately for each Transaction and for each fee component, and is rounded to the nearest whole euro cent before fees are aggregated on the Invoice. An amount of exactly half a cent is rounded up, away from zero.

## 5. Refunds

**5.1 Refund fee and markup credit.** (a) For each Refund the Merchant shall pay a refund fee of EUR 0.10 (ten euro cents).

(b) On each Refund the Acquirer shall credit the Merchant with the Markup under clause 4.2 on the refunded Successful Transaction. The Interchange Fee, the Scheme Fee, the fixed fee under clause 4.3 and any other fee on that Transaction are not credited.

## 6. Chargebacks

**6.1 Chargeback fee.** For each Chargeback the Merchant shall pay a chargeback handling fee of EUR 15.00 (fifteen euro), whatever the outcome of any representment.

## 7. Invoicing, disputes and minimum fee

**7.1 Monthly invoice.** Within ten Business Days after the end of each calendar month the Acquirer shall issue an Invoice for the fees of that month, together with a statement listing each fee for each Transaction.

**7.2 Payment.** Each Invoice is payable within fourteen days of its date. The Acquirer may instead deduct the amount of an Invoice from funds it holds for the Merchant, provided the Invoice has been issued first.

**7.3 Disputes.** The Merchant may dispute an Invoice, or any fee or charge shown on it or on the accompanying statement, by written notice given within sixty (60) days after the date of the Invoice. Any amount found to have been overcharged shall be credited on the next Invoice. Fees not disputed within that period are deemed accepted, except in the case of manifest error or fraud.

**7.4 Monthly minimum.** If the total Markup under clause 4.2 on the Successful Transactions of a calendar month is less than EUR 2,500.00 (two thousand five hundred euro), the Merchant shall pay the difference as a minimum fee for that month, shown as a separate line on the Invoice. Markup credits on Refunds under clause 5.1 are disregarded for this purpose.

## 8. General

**8.1 Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes any earlier arrangement.

**8.2 Variation.** No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is made in writing and signed by or on behalf of both parties, including by an amendment letter from the Acquirer countersigned by the Merchant. An amendment letter states the date from which it applies.

**8.3 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany. The courts of Frankfurt am Main have exclusive jurisdiction.

## Schedule 1: Reference Table

The Reference Table "interchange_table_v1" sets out the Interchange Fee and the Scheme Fee by Card Scheme, card type and region. It is maintained by the Acquirer, made available to the Merchant on request and incorporated in this Agreement by reference.
