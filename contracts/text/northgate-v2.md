# Amendment No. 1 to the Master Acquiring Services Agreement

**From:** Northgate Acquiring (the "Acquirer")

**To:** Fernway (the "Merchant")

**Date:** 11 March 2026

**Agreement reference:** NG-2025-01

Dear Sir or Madam,

**Master Acquiring Services Agreement dated 1 July 2025, reference NG-2025-01 (the "Agreement")**

We refer to the Agreement. Terms defined in the Agreement have the same meaning in this letter, and references to clauses are to clauses of the Agreement. This letter is Amendment No. 1 to the Agreement and is made under clause 8.2.

1. **Amendment Effective Date.** The amendments in paragraph 2 take effect on 1 April 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date") and apply to all Transactions made on or after that date, determined in accordance with clause 1.2. Transactions made before the Amendment Effective Date remain subject to the Agreement as it stood before this letter.

2. **Amendments.** With effect from the Amendment Effective Date:

(a) clause 4.2 is deleted and replaced by the following:

> **4.2 Markup.** (a) For each Successful Transaction the Merchant shall pay a Markup equal to the percentage of the Transaction amount shown below for the band into which the Monthly Processed Volume of the calendar month of the Transaction falls:
>
> | Band | Monthly Processed Volume | Markup |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | less than EUR 2,500,000.00 | 0.45% (forty-five basis points) |
> | 2 | EUR 2,500,000.00 or more but less than EUR 8,000,000.00 | 0.32% (thirty-two basis points) |
> | 3 | EUR 8,000,000.00 or more | 0.28% (twenty-eight basis points) |
>
> (b) The band is determined once the Monthly Processed Volume of the month is known, and its rate applies to every Successful Transaction of that month, including Transactions processed before the volume reached the lower limit of that band.
>
> (c) The Markup is subject to the monthly minimum in clause 7.4 and rounded in accordance with clause 4.4.

3. **Continuation.** Except as amended by this letter, the Agreement continues in full force and effect, and every other clause, including the fees, the rounding rules and the dispute period, applies unchanged. From the Amendment Effective Date, references to the Agreement are to the Agreement as amended by this letter.

4. **Governing law.** This letter is governed by the law that governs the Agreement.

Please countersign this letter to confirm the Merchant's agreement.
